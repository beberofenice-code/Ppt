Definition of spiritual self.

Attending religious gatherings.

Reading spiritual or religious teachings.

Building positive relationships with others.

Learning patience, acceptance, and understanding.

Finding meaning and purpose in life.

Summary of the importance of the spiritual self.

How spirituality contributes to personal growth and well-being.

The importance of respecting different beliefs and religions.

Final realization: Spirituality helps individuals develop a meaningful life guided by faith, values, and

purpose.
