## III. Visual Theme

### Theme Style

- **Mode**: custom
- **Mode References**: instructional
- **Mode Behavior**: Follow the user's outline order exactly; each point slide states the outline point as its title and explains it in 2–3 plain academic sentences, with optional 2–4 keyword cues; roman-numeral section dividers reset attention between Introduction, Body and Conclusion; the deck closes on the outline's final realization as the takeaway.
- **Visual style**: custom
- **Visual Style References**: swiss-minimal
- **Visual Style Behavior**: Pure white field with generous margins and a left-aligned grid; each title carries a short teal accent bar; explanations sit as large dark text on white; keyword cues are light-tinted rounded tiles with solid navy/teal icons; dividers use an oversized pale roman numeral; ornament limited to a small section label at top-left and page number at bottom-right; no shadows, no gradients.
- **Theme**: Calm, scholarly, readable from a distance
- **Tone**: Respectful, neutral across beliefs, warm

Cross-page motif: a short teal title bar plus a small gold dot — recurs on every point slide as title marker; on dividers it scales up beside the numeral.
